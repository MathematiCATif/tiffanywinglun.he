The Solution to Schubert’s Problem and Convex Polyhedra

Tiffany Wing Lun He

SummaryThis paper develops a topological treatment of Schubert’s problem about lines intersecting four given lines in three-dimensional space. Using Morse theory and the cohomology of the Grassmannian, it explains the generic count and gives a geometric interpretation connected to a regular octahedron.
