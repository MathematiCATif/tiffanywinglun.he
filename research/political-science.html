Be Skeptical of Lies

Tiffany Wing Lun He

Essay summaryThis essay examines whether politicians who lie should be punished. It distinguishes deliberate lies, truth-indifferent “bullshit,” and culpable errors, arguing that each can weaken public trust. Drawing on Rawls’s account of political stability, it argues against blanket punishment and favors proportionate accountability, transparency, public explanation, and restorative approaches that can rebuild trust without deepening political fear and cynicism.
