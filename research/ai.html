Modern Renaissance—The Restoration and Artistic Creation of Ancient Architecture Based on Multimodal and 3D Modeling Technologies

Tiffany Wing Lun He

Project summaryThis project explores how multimodal AI and 3D modeling can support the restoration and creative reinterpretation of damaged historic architecture. It describes assembling and organizing images of Eastern and Western architecture, extracting structural outlines, and developing a two-stage model to identify damaged areas and recover visual details. Planned outputs include reconstructed 2D images, 3D models, and AI-assisted restoration reports.

ApproachThe paper describes image processing with OpenCV, architectural line extraction using U²-Net, and a reconstruction workflow combining neural-network components and multimodal language-model guidance.
